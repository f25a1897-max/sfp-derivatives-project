Application of Derivatives in Agro Product Technology

This project demonstrates how derivatives f'(x) are applied in a realistic Malaysian bioscience scenario involving the drying process of mango slices. In Agro Product Technology, drying is important to preserve food quality, reduce moisture, and extend shelf life.

The graph below models the moisture content of mango slices during hot air drying. By using derivatives, we can determine how fast moisture is lost at a specific time.

Bioscience Problem

Mango drying is commonly used in Malaysian agro-food industries. During drying, moisture decreases continuously over time.

Food scientists use derivatives to measure the rate of moisture loss. This helps industries optimize drying efficiency and maintain food quality.

Faster drying may reduce processing time, while slower drying helps maintain texture and nutrients.

Mathematical Model

Moisture Function:

f(x) = 80e-0.15x + 10

Where:

  • x = drying time (hours)
  • f(x) = moisture content (%)

Derivative Function:

f'(x) = -12e-0.15x

The derivative represents the rate of moisture loss per hour.
